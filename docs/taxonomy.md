# TTRPG Grand Unified Taxonomy (GUT) v3.5
**Version**: 3.5
**Scope**: System Agnostic (Fantasy, Sci-Fi, Horror, Modern)  
**Purpose**: To strictly categorize unstructured RPG text into a standardized hierarchical schema.

> **Schema Definition**: This/these taxonomies define the **Fields** (Tag Categories) and **Values** (Suggested Tags) for each structural folder. This ensures consistent data entry.

## VI. UNIVERSAL METADATA
Tags that apply to all entries in the taxonomy, regardless of category.
**Tag Schema**:
*   **Source Book**:
    *   *Values*: Core Rulebook, Splatbook, Homebrew, Adventure Module
*   **Game System**:
    *   *Values*: D&D 5e, Pathfinder, ShadowDark, Cyberpunk Red, System Agnostic
*   **Version**:
    *   *Values*: v1.0, 2024 Edition, Alpha

## I. ACTORS (Living Entities)
Active agents in the game world with agency, behavior, and statistics.

### 1. Monsters (Bestiary)
Hostile or neutral entities primarily defined by a stat block.
**Tag Schema**:
*   **Family** (Biological Origin):
    *   *Values*: Humanoid, Beast, Undead, Construct, Outsider, Monstrosity, Plant
*   **Rank** (Power Level):
    *   *Values*: Minion, Standard, Elite, Boss, Solo
*   **Biome** (Location):
    *   *Values*: Forest, Dungeon, Urban, Aquatic, Desert

### 2. NPCs (Dramatis Personae)
Named or unique characters primarily defined by social role.
**Tag Schema**:
*   **Social Role**:
    *   *Values*: Ally, Patron, Rival, Commoner
*   **Disposition**:
    *   *Values*: Friendly, Hostile, Neutral, Unaligned

### 3. Archetypes (PC Options)
Templates used by players to construct characters.
**Tag Schema**:
*   **Definition Type**:
    *   *Values*: Class, Subclass, Ancestry, Background

## II. OBJECTS (Tangible Assets)
Physical things that can be owned, traded, or destroyed.

### 4. Equipment (Personal Scale)
Items carried on a person.

#### Level 2: Weapons
Instruments of combat.
**Tag Schema**:
*   **Usage Category**:
    *   *Values*: Melee, Ranged, Thrown, Ammunition
*   **Physical Form**:
    *   *Values*: Bladed, Bludgeoning, Polearm, Bow, Firearm, Energy
*   **Properties**:
    *   *Values*: Finesse, Reach, Loading, Versatile, Light, Heavy

#### Level 2: Armor
Protective gear and shields.
**Tag Schema**:
*   **Armor Class**:
    *   *Values*: Light, Medium, Heavy, Powered
*   **Form Factor**:
    *   *Values*: Body Armor, Shield, Barrier
*   **Material**:
    *   *Values*: Leather, Chain, Plate, Synth, Energy

#### Level 2: Gear
Tools, kits, and utility items.
**Tag Schema**:
*   **Usage Domain**:
    *   *Values*: Survival, Trade Tool, Thievery, Tech, Magical Focus
*   **Item Type**:
    *   *Values*: Kit, Gadget, Implement, Container

#### Level 2: Accessories
Worn items that provide passive benefits or flavor.
**Tag Schema**:
*   **Body Slot**:
    *   *Values*: Neck, Ring, Head, Body, Cyberware, Tattoo, Hands, Feet

#### Level 2: Consumables
Single-use items.
**Tag Schema**:
*   **Primary Effect**:
    *   *Values*: Restorative, Hazardous, Utility, Enhanced
*   **Form**:
    *   *Values*: Potion, Scroll, Explosive, Toxin, Med-kit, Ration

#### Level 2: Valuables
Items primarily for trade or wealth storage.
**Tag Schema**:
*   **Type**:
    *   *Values*: Currency, Art Object, Commodity, Trade Good, Gemstone

### 5. Assets (Macro Scale)
Large-scale possessions.

#### Level 2: Vehicles
Mobile assets.
**Tag Schema**:
*   **Environment**:
    *   *Values*: Land, Water, Air, Space
*   **Form**:
    *   *Values*: Mount, Ship, Craft, Mech, Wagon

#### Level 2: Structures
Immobile physical assets.
**Tag Schema**:
*   **Function**:
    *   *Values*: Real Estate, Infrastructure, Base, Shop, Temple

#### Level 2: Enterprise
Abstract social assets.
**Tag Schema**:
*   **Type**:
    *   *Values*: Organization, Route, Guild, Faction

## III. MECHANICS (Game Systems)
Rules, abilities, and abstract interactions.

### 6. Powers (Abilities)
Active abilities used by Actors (Spells, Tech, etc.).
**Tag Schema**:
*   **Power Source**:
    *   *Values*: Arcane, Divine, Primal, Tech, Psionic, Martial
*   **School/Discipline**:
    *   *Values*: Evocation, Illusion, Necromancy, Combat, Hacking
*   **Class Limit**:
    *   *Values*: Wizard, Cleric, Hacker, Fighter
*   **Tier/Level**:
    *   *Values*: Cantrip, Level 1st-9th, Rank 1-5, Tier 1-5

### 7. Features (Passives)
Passive qualities, permanent upgrades, or status effects.
**Tag Schema**:
*   **Origin Source**:
    *   *Values*: Ancestry, Class, Feat, Boon, Affliction
*   **Interaction Type**:
    *   *Values*: Passive, Reaction, Status Effect
*   **Condition Name**:
    *   *Values*: Blinded, Prone, Blessed, Exhausted

## IV. CONTEXT (World Data)
Setting information.

### 8. Atlas (Locations)
**Tag Schema**:
*   **Scale**: Region, Site, Zone
*   **Type**: Biome, Settlement, Dungeon, Room

### 9. Plot (Narrative)
**Tag Schema**:
*   **Type**: Quest, Rumor, Secret, Faction

## V. TABLES (Generators)
**Tag Schema**:
*   **Output Type**: Encounter, Loot, Narrative, Flavor
